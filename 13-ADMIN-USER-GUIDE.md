# 13. Admin User Guide

## 1. Sign In
Open the admin page through the supported HTTP server and authenticate with an authorized account.

## 2. Dashboard
Start from the dashboard to review KPIs and operational notifications.

## 3. Orders
Open Orders to search, inspect and export invoice/order information.

## 4. Customers
Search for a customer, open the customer record and review their order, item, warranty and review information.

## 5. Catalogue
Use Catalogue to maintain product information and export catalogue data.

## 6. Inventory
Use Inventory to identify low and out-of-stock items and inspect stock information.

## 7. Reviews
Review customer feedback, ratings and replies. Use moderation controls according to the configured role.

## 8. Coupons
Create and manage coupon codes and monitor expiry/usage.

## 9. Reports
Generate sales reports and workbook exports.

## 10. Activity
Review audit/activity history to understand administrative changes.

## 11. Settings
Configure appearance and accessibility options.

## 12. Data
Use data administration carefully. Destructive operations require confirmation and cannot be assumed reversible.
