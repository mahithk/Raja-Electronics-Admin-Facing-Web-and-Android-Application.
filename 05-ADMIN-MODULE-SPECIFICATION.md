# 5. Admin Module Specification

## Dashboard
KPIs, alerts, order status, stock status and operational summaries.

## Orders & Invoices
Order list, detail view, status, payment state, totals, customer information and export.

## Customers
Customer search, profile information, order history, purchased units, warranty and review information.

## Catalogue
Product CRUD, pricing, GST, HSN, warranty, stock visibility and export.

## Inventory
Stock ledger, low/out-of-stock monitoring and quantity management.

## Sold Units & Warranty
Serial tracking, invoice linkage, customer linkage, warranty status and expiry.

## Reviews
Review listing, rating, text, replies and moderation state.

## Coupons
Coupon creation/maintenance, usage and expiry state.

## Reports
Sales summaries and downloadable business reports.

## Data
Scoped deletion, reset operations and storage inspection where enabled.

## Settings
Appearance, accessibility and shop/admin configuration.

## Search
Global search across live business data without maintaining a stale cache.
